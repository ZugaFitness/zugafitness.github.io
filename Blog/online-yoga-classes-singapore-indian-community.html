Online Yoga Classes in Singapore — Live from India, Just 2.5 Hours Ahead (2026)

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Finding online yoga classes singapore residents can actually stick with comes down to three things: a teacher who understands you, a schedule that fits your workday, and a format that keeps you honest. For the city's large Indian community, all three point in one direction — live classes with certified teachers in India, streamed into your HDB or condo living room at times that match Singapore hours. No commute to a pricey studio, no recorded videos gathering dust, no losing your practice to travel.

Reviewed by Anusha, Certified Yoga & Zumba Instructor, Zuga Fitness

Why Singapore's Indian Community Practices Live with Teachers from India

  • Cultural and language comfort: Instructions that understand your diet, your festivals, your family schedule — and can switch between Hindi, Kannada, Tamil and English mid-cue when needed.
  • Authentic lineage: Teachers trained in India in Hatha, Vinyasa and Pranayama, bringing traditional depth that generic boutique studios often skip.
  • Cost sense: Singapore studio drop-in rates are among the highest in Asia. Live online classes deliver personal attention at a fraction of that.
  • Community: You practice alongside other Indian expats across Singapore, the Gulf, and beyond — the warmth of a neighbourhood class, without the lift wait.

The Timezone Advantage — SGT Is Only 2.5 Hours Ahead of IST

Singapore sits just 2.5 hours ahead of Indian Standard Time, which makes live scheduling almost effortless. All sessions are booked in your local time:

Indian Standard Time (IST) Singapore Time (SGT) Best For
7:00 AM IST 9:30 AM SGT Mid-morning reset for WFH professionals
4:30 PM IST 7:00 PM SGT Post-work decompression before dinner
6:30 PM IST 9:00 PM SGT Evening wind-down for better sleep

What a Live Session Actually Looks Like

Every class runs live on Zoom with cameras on. Your instructor watches your alignment in real time — correcting a collapsing knee in Warrior II or a rounding back in Downward Dog before it becomes an injury. You can ask questions before and after class, and you practice inside a standing community of expat students, not a anonymous stream. All you need is a mat, a device, and two square metres of floor.

Group Class or 1-on-1?

Feature Live Group Classes 1-on-1 Personal Training
Schedule Fixed SGT slots Fully flexible, built around travel
Feedback Personal corrections in class Fully individualized programming
Best For Community energy and consistency Shift work, frequent flyers, specific goals

Group classes carry the community energy that keeps habits alive. But if your week involves Changi departures, shift rotations, or a specific goal like back-pain relief or PCOD management, an online personal yoga trainer builds the program around your body and calendar — with WhatsApp support between sessions.

Trainer's note: My Singapore students often tell me the same thing: between the heat outside and the air-con inside, plus long office hours, their bodies feel "locked" by evening. Twenty minutes of live movement and breathwork unlocks that. And because the class is live and social, they actually show up — week after week, festival seasons and all.

FAQs

Are online yoga classes from India to Singapore live or recorded?
100% live on Zoom, in real time, with personal corrections. Recordings are never sold as a substitute for coaching.

What time are yoga classes in Singapore?
Sessions are scheduled directly in SGT - typically 9:30 AM, 7:00 PM and 9:00 PM slots - so you never do timezone math.

Can my family join from Malaysia or India on the same plan?
Yes. Family plans let members in different countries join live sessions across timezones. Ask our team for details.

Is online yoga good for beginners in Singapore?
Yes - beginner batches move slowly with step-by-step cues and safe modifications, ideal if you have never practiced before.

Final Thoughts

A consistent practice in Singapore does not require an expensive studio membership or a battle with the humidity. It requires a live teacher, a sane schedule, and a mat at home.
