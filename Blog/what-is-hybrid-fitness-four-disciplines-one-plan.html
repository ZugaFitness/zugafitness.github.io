Four disciplines, one weekly plan

The word hybrid describes a plan that combines more than one training discipline. At Zuga Fitness, the weekly rotation includes strength and conditioning, dance fitness, yoga and pranayama. Each class has a distinct focus, while the schedule brings them together in a single membership.

Why a rotation can make a routine easier to follow

Repeating one kind of session can become difficult to sustain when your schedule, energy or interests shift. A rotation gives members different ways to take part during the week and can help keep a routine engaging. Begin with a pace that fits your experience and make adjustments with guidance from your instructor.

Live instruction and recorded workouts

During a live class, an instructor leads the session and participants can interact as they move. Recorded workouts offer flexibility to press play at a convenient time, while a live class provides real-time group energy and coaching.
