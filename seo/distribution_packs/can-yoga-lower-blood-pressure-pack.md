Can Yoga Lower Blood Pressure? What the Evidence Says (2026)

The short answer is yes. Yoga has been shown to produce modest but real reductions in blood pressure. However, it's not a magic cure-all. Yoga lowers blood pressure primarily through three distinct pathways: reducing stress hormones, improving breath-driven vagal tone, and enhancing arterial function.

Chronic stress keeps the body's fight-or-flight response activated, constantly pumping cortisol and adrenaline into the system, which constricts blood vessels. Slow, intentional breathing practiced in yoga directly stimulates the vagus nerve. This activation switches the nervous system into its "rest and digest" parasympathetic state, which dilates blood vessels and lowers the resting heart rate.
