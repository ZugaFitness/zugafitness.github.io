Refund Policy

Class packs: unused sessions are refundable pro-rata within 90 days of purchase, minus attended sessions at the single-class rate; packs expire at their stated validity.

Memberships: cancel anytime by email, effective at the end of the paid period; the first 7 days of a first membership are fully refundable if you attended no more than 2 classes.

Cohorts and programmes (Weight Loss Challenge, PCOS, Postpartum): full refund within 7 days of start if you attended 2 or fewer sessions; thereafter pro-rata for unstarted weeks, excluding materials already delivered.

Single classes: reschedulable up to 12 hours before start; no-shows are not refundable.

Onsite and corporate bookings: per contract - 50% deposit on confirmation; free cancellation more than 72 hours before the session; 50% within 72 hours; emergency deployment surcharge is non-refundable once deployed.

Processing: approved refunds reach the original payment method in 5-7 business days. Request via hello@zugafitness.in with your order details; we respond within 3 business days.

Exceptions: medical emergencies are considered case by case with documentation; instructor-cancelled sessions are always refunded or credited in full. Last updated: 2026-11-01
