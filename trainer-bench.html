Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide equipment for onsite sessions?

We do not supply mats. However, we specialize in "Desk Yoga" and "Chair Zumba" which require zero equipment and can be done in everyday office wear right at the workstation or in a standard conference room.

How do you handle branding when working under an agency?

Our trainers represent your agency. We do not distribute Zuga promotional materials during the session. All post-session reports can accommodate your agency logo.

What if our client wants to increase the frequency of sessions?

Per our strict non-circumvention policy, any upsell or frequency increase is routed directly back to you. We do not bill the end client directly.
