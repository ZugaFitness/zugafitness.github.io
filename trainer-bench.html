Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide equipment for onsite sessions?

We do not supply mats. However, we specialize in "Desk Yoga" and "Chair Zumba" which require zero equipment and can be done in everyday office wear right at the workstation or in a standard conference room.

How do you handle branding when working under an agency?

Our trainers represent your agency. We do not distribute Zuga promotional materials during the session. All post-session reports can accommodate your agency logo.

What if our client wants to increase the frequency of sessions?

Per our strict non-circumvention policy, any upsell or frequency increase is routed directly back to you. We do not bill the end client directly.

Common Questions

How fast can you deploy a trainer for a corporate event? +

Standard bookings are confirmed within 48 hours anywhere in India, same-day in Bangalore where our core team is based. You get a named instructor and a single point of contact from briefing to wrap-up.

What happens if a trainer falls sick on event day? +

A backup instructor is on standby for every confirmed booking and deploys at no extra cost to you or your client. The standby policy is exactly why our calendar has no gaps.

How does billing work for agencies? +

We invoice your agency at agreed partner rates with a GST-compliant invoice; you bill your client at your own margin. The non-circumvention clause protects that margin in writing.
