We are Zuga Fitness, Bengaluru, India. Contact: hello@zugafitness.in / +91 76763 79909.

What we collect: your name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, city and timezone; any health, pregnancy or injury information you choose to share in booking or intake forms so we can keep sessions safe; payment records handled by our payment provider (we never store card details); attendance and feedback notes from your sessions.

How we use it: to schedule and deliver your classes; to screen for safety and adapt sessions; to send session links, schedules and practice notes you opted into; to produce participation reports for corporate bookings (attendance, department split and quotes, anonymised unless you agree otherwise); to improve our classes; and to meet legal obligations.

What we never do: we do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it except with service processors (video conferencing, payments, WhatsApp, email, analytics) bound by confidentiality.

Cookies and analytics: we use Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics and Meta Pixel to understand site usage. You can block these in your browser settings.

Retention: booking records for 3 years; health disclosures only as long as needed for session safety and deleted on request; marketing consent until you withdraw it.

Your rights: access, correction, deletion, export and withdrawal of consent - email hello@zugafitness.in and we will respond within 30 days.

Children: classes are for ages 16+; children's sessions require parental consent and supervision.

Changes: updates are posted on this page with a new date.
