The 3-Week Rotation Method

“Variety is the foundation of lasting fitness. We never let your body plateau.”

— The Zuga Hybrid Fitness Method

Our Hybrid Fitness program intelligently rotates your weekly focus to build strength, cardiovascular endurance, and mobility without burnout. Every month follows this proven 3-week cycle:

  • Week 1: 2 days Strength & Conditioning, 2 days Dance Fitness, 1 day Yoga.
  • Week 2: 2 days Strength & Conditioning, 1 day Dance Fitness, 2 days Yoga.
  • Week 3: 1 day Strength & Conditioning, 2 days Dance Fitness, 2 days Yoga.

(This cycle repeats monthly to ensure balanced, holistic progress.)
